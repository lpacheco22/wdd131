Welcome to Ecuador

Ecuador is one of South America's most diverse countries. Despite its small size, it offers visitors an incredible variety of natural landscapes, cultural traditions, and unforgettable adventures.

From the colonial streets of Quito to the wildlife of the Galápagos Islands, Ecuador provides unique experiences for travelers seeking nature, history, and culture.
